================================================================
EMS STUDY — PARTICIPANT INFORMATION & INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================

Thank you for taking part in this study. Please read this
sheet carefully before we begin.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS STUDY IS ABOUT
----------------------------------------------------------------
We have built a computer system that converts the way a person
moves their mouse and types on a keyboard into sound, in
real-time. The sounds change depending on how the person is
feeling — whether they are stressed or calm.

In this study, you will listen to short audio recordings made
by this system and tell us what you hear.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU WILL DO
----------------------------------------------------------------
You will hear 8 audio recordings, each lasting 60 seconds.

After each recording, you will:

  1. Rate the sound on two scales:

     AROUSAL — How energetic / activated did the sound feel?
        1 = Very calm / sleepy
        9 = Very energetic / excited

     VALENCE — How pleasant / positive did the sound feel?
        1 = Very unpleasant / negative
        9 = Very pleasant / positive

  2. Make a guess:
     "Do you think the person who generated this sound was
      STRESSED or RELAXED?"
     Circle one: STRESSED / RELAXED

  3. Rate your confidence:
        1 = Just guessing
        3 = Somewhat confident
        5 = Very confident

----------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Use headphones throughout the study.
- Listen to the full 60 seconds before rating.
- There are no right or wrong answers — we want your honest
  first impression.
- Please do not talk during the recordings.
- You may ask questions at any time.

----------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR RIGHTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Your participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time.
- Your responses are anonymous. No personal data is collected.
- Results will be used for academic research only.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TRAINING TRIALS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Before the main study, you will hear 2 practice recordings.
These are just to help you get familiar with the rating process.
Your responses to the practice trials will NOT be analysed.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Please sign the consent form when you are ready to begin.
